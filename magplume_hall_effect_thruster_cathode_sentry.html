1. Sheath Transit-Time Oscillation Frequency

In the cathode hollow keeper orifice, thermalized electrons are extracted across a collisionless plasma sheath. The high-frequency noise spikes ($f$) originate from acoustic ions transiting the Debye sheath length $s$ under the Bohm velocity criterion:

$$f_{\text{transit}} \approx \frac{v_{\text{Bohm}}}{2\pi s} = \frac{1}{2\pi \lambda_D}\sqrt{\frac{k_B T_e}{M_i}} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{e^2 n_e}{\varepsilon_0 M_i}}$$

When the local magnetic shielding topology is undisturbed, cross-field diffusion restricts electron back-streaming, keeping local density $n_e$ high and pushing peak transit modes above $85 \text{ kHz}$.

$$B_0(T) = B_{\text{nom}} \left[ 1 - \alpha_{\text{mag}} (T - T_0) \right] \cdot \left[ \frac{R_0}{R_0 \left(1 + \alpha_{\text{Cu}}(T - T_0)\right)} \right]$$

When cross-field impedance drops, high-frequency turbulence undergoes an inverse energy cascade into lower-frequency ion acoustic and spoke-instability modes. Ion bombardment energy against the cathode keeper electrode ($E_{\text{ion}}$) scales inversely with oscillation frequency:

$$Y_{\text{erosion}} \propto \Phi_{\text{ion}} \left( \frac{f_{\text{nom}}}{f_{\text{instability}}} \right)^2 \cdot \exp\left( \frac{e(V_{\text{plasma}} - V_{\text{keeper}})}{k_B T_i} \right)$$
